What is a Lookback Campaign?
When a repeat donor tests reactive for HIV, HCV, HBV or other transfusion-transmissible infections on a new donation, all previous donations within the pathogen window period must be traced. If any units from those donations were transfused, the recipient patients must be notified via their treating clinicians for testing.

Window periods (conservative): HIV ≈45 days · HCV ≈70 days · HBV ≈82 days · HTLV ≈51 days · Syphilis ≈30 days.
