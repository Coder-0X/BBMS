Legal Notice — Blood Bank Data Retention
Blood bank traceability records are subject to a 30-year mandatory retention period under EU Blood Directive 2002/98/EC (Annex VI). The right to erasure under GDPR Article 17 does not apply to these records (GDPR Art 17(3)(b)/(c)). Erasure requests from donors with donation records will be legally refused for blood-safety data; marketing data and contact information will still be removed.
