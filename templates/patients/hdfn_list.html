HDFN occurs when maternal alloantibodies cross the placenta and destroy fetal/neonatal red cells. Severe cases require exchange transfusion or intrauterine transfusion. All exchange transfusion cases should be reported to your national haemovigilance system (SHOT/EUFEPT). References: BCSH 2014, NICE CG133, BSH HDFN 2016.
