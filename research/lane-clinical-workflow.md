# Lane: LANE 1 — TRANSFUSION CLINICAL WORKFLOW

## Executive summary
A sellable transfusion system must implement the full pretransfusion safety chain, not just an ABO/Rh lookup: a Type & Screen (T&S) sample with a defined validity window, an antibody (alloantibody) screen against a reagent panel, and a documented crossmatch (serologic immediate-spin and/or AHG, or electronic/computer crossmatch when strict criteria are met). It must support emergency uncrossmatched group O release (O-neg RBC, often AB plasma) with retrospective testing and physician sign-off, and encode an MSBOS so requests default to T&S vs crossmatch by procedure to reduce waste. Component production must model a separation/processing event that splits one whole-blood (or apheresis) collection into multiple child products (LR-RBC, FFP, platelets, cryoprecipitate) with full parent→child lineage so any product traces back to the donor and forward to the recipient. Special attributes (irradiated, leukoreduced, CMV-negative, washed, frozen/deglycerolized, antigen-negative) must be first-class, clinically-gated unit properties that the issue logic enforces. ABO/Rh static lookup is unsafe because it ignores clinically significant non-ABO alloantibodies, historical/repeat-sample ABO confirmation, sample validity expiry, antigen-negative requirements, and patient-specific special needs — all of which the crossmatch and antibody records exist to catch.

## Standards referenced
- AABB Standards for Blood Banks and Transfusion Services (33rd ed.) — pretransfusion testing, ABO double determination, electronic crossmatch eligibility, irradiation, sample validity
- AABB/ICCBBA Circular of Information for the Use of Human Blood and Blood Components — component indications and special attributes
- ISBT 128 (ICCBBA ST-001) — Donation Identification Number (DIN), product description codes, component labeling and lineage
- FDA 21 CFR 606 (cGMP for blood), 610 (general biological standards), 640 (blood component requirements), 606.160 (records/traceability)
- FDA 21 CFR Part 11 — electronic records and electronic signatures (validation of electronic crossmatch / e-signoff)
- EU Directive 2002/98/EC and 2005/61/EC — traceability ('vein to vein'), 30-year record retention, hemovigilance/serious adverse reaction reporting
- British Society for Haematology (BSH/BCSH) Guidelines for pretransfusion compatibility procedures and for the use of irradiated/CMV-negative components
- AABB Technical Manual — antibody screen/ID methods (IAT, LISS, PEG, gel), immediate-spin vs AHG crossmatch, MSBOS and C:T ratio

## Recommendations
- **[must-have] Implement Type & Screen with sample validity window and ABO confirmation rule** — Add a patient_pretransfusion_sample (specimen) entity capturing: ABO group, Rh(D) type, antibody screen result (neg/pos), draw datetime, collector ID, and a computed validity_expiry. Per AABB Standard 5.something / common practice: if the patient has been transfused or pregnant within the preceding 3 months (or history unknown), the crossmatch/compatibility sample is valid only 3 days (sample drawn day = day 0; expires end of day 3). Otherwise institutions may extend. ABO/Rh must be determined on the current sample AND confirmed against a second determination (historical record OR second independently-drawn sample) before non-emergency group-specific issue — this is the AABB requirement to prevent wrong-blood-in-tube (WBIT). System must BLOCK group-specific issue if only a single ABO determination exists with no historical confirmation.
- **[must-have] Model antibody (alloantibody) screen and identification as records, not booleans** — Add antibody_screen (per specimen: method IAT/PEG/LISS/gel, reagent screen cell lot, result per cell, overall pos/neg, tech ID) and antibody_identification (panel) records. When positive, store identified specificities (e.g., anti-K, anti-E, anti-Fya, anti-Jka) as a structured, persistent patient antibody history (transfusion_antibody_history) that survives across visits and branches — antibodies can evaniesce below detection but still cause hemolysis, so a once-identified antibody must permanently flag the patient and force antigen-negative + AHG crossmatched units thereafter. This persistent record is exactly what a static ABO/Rh table cannot represent.
- **[must-have] Implement crossmatch as a typed, evidenced event with three methods** — Add crossmatch record linked to (patient_sample_id, blood_unit_id, ordering_request_id) capturing: method = immediate_spin (IS, ABO/major incompatibility detection only), AHG/full antiglobulin (IAT — required when current or historical clinically significant antibody present), or electronic/computer crossmatch; result (compatible/incompatible); tech ID + timestamp; reagent/lot; and unit segment used. ELECTRONIC CROSSMATCH eligibility must be system-enforced and is permitted ONLY when ALL hold: (a) two concordant ABO/Rh determinations on the patient (one current), (b) current antibody screen negative AND no history of clinically significant antibodies, (c) the computer system has been validated to alert on ABO incompatibility, and (d) donor unit ABO/Rh confirmed by the facility. If any condition fails, the system must downgrade to serologic AHG crossmatch. Hard-code these gates; do not allow override without supervisor e-signature.
- **[must-have] Emergency uncrossmatched (massive transfusion) release with retrospective workflow** — Add an emergency_release pathway: allow issue of group O RBC (O-neg for females of childbearing potential / unknown; O-pos acceptable for males/older to conserve O-neg) and group AB or low-titer A plasma BEFORE crossmatch/screen complete, requiring an ordering physician identity + emergency justification captured at release (cannot be silently bypassed). System must then create mandatory open follow-up tasks: complete ABO/Rh + antibody screen + retrospective crossmatch on the pre-transfusion sample, and a physician sign-off acknowledging uncrossmatched release. Units issued emergency-uncrossmatched must remain flagged in the audit trail. Per AABB the retrospective compatibility testing must still be completed even though transfusion preceded it.
- **[should-have] Encode MSBOS to drive request defaults and the crossmatch:transfusion (C:T) ratio** — Add msbos table keyed by procedure (and optionally branch) → recommended action: none / T&S only / crossmatch N units. blood_requests should default order type from MSBOS, steering low-bleeding-risk procedures to T&S (no units reserved) rather than holding crossmatched units. Track and report C:T ratio (units crossmatched ÷ units transfused); target ≈ ≤2.0:1. This is both a clinical-safety and inventory-efficiency selling point and directly reduces reservation-lock contention and outdating.
- **[must-have] Model component production as a separation event with parent→child lineage** — Add component_production (processing) event: input = source collection unit (whole blood or apheresis), output = N child blood_units each with its own ISBT 128 DIN+product code, derived product type, volume, additive solution, and inherited donor/infectious-marker results. One whole-blood unit → typically 1 LR-RBC (in additive solution) + 1 FFP (frozen ≤8h for FFP, ≤24h = FP24) + 1 platelet (from buffy-coat/PRP), and cryoprecipitate is produced by controlled thaw of FFP → 1 cryo + cryo-poor plasma. blood_units needs parent_unit_id (self-referential), production_event_id, and product_code. Lineage must be bidirectional: forward trace (donor→components→recipients, for lookback on a later-positive donor) and backward trace (recipient reaction→unit→donor). This is mandated by EU Directive 2002/98/EC traceability ('vein to vein', 30-year retention) and FDA 21 CFR 606.160.
- **[must-have] Make special attributes first-class, clinically-gated unit properties enforced at issue** — Add boolean/typed attributes on blood_units: leukoreduced (LR — most cellular products by default; reduces febrile non-hemolytic reactions, HLA alloimmunization, CMV risk), irradiated (gamma/X-ray, prevents transfusion-associated GVHD — required for cellular products to immunocompromised, intrauterine/neonatal exchange, HLA-matched, directed donations from relatives, Hodgkin/hematopoietic transplant patients; irradiation shortens RBC dating to 28 days from irradiation or original expiry, whichever sooner), CMV_negative (donor seronegative — for CMV-safe needs e.g. intrauterine, some neonates, CMV-seronegative pregnant, transplant; LR is often accepted as 'CMV-safe' equivalent), washed (removes plasma — IgA deficiency with anti-IgA, severe allergic/anaphylactic reactions, neonatal where K+ load matters), frozen/deglycerolized (rare/antigen-negative RBC long-term storage; post-deglyc 24h outdate), antigen_negative (specific antigens e.g. K-neg, E-neg, Jka-neg). Add patient_special_requirements per patient; issue engine must MATCH unit attributes to patient requirements and BLOCK issue of a non-conforming unit (e.g., non-irradiated to an irradiation-required patient) with hard stop + override audit.
- **[must-have] Two-person/barcode bedside administration check and transfusion outcome capture** — Add bedside verification (scan patient wristband DIN + unit DIN, confirm match independently) and transfusion_administration record (start/stop time, vitals, administering staff). Add transfusion_reaction record (type: AHTR/DHTR/FNHTR/allergic/TACO/TRALI/septic, severity, workup) feeding back to the patient antibody/special-requirements history and to hemovigilance reporting. Wrong-blood-in-tube and mis-transfusion are the dominant fatal errors; barcode verification is the single highest-value safety control.

## Data model changes
- blood_units: add parent_unit_id (self-FK), production_event_id (FK), product_code (ISBT 128), additive_solution, and attribute columns leukoreduced/irradiated/irradiation_datetime/cmv_negative/washed/deglycerolized/antigen_negative_set; recompute expiry on irradiation (28d) and deglycerolization (24h)
- component_production (NEW): id, source_unit_id (FK→blood_units), event_datetime, operator_id, method, validated flag; produces multiple child blood_units
- patient_pretransfusion_sample / specimen (NEW): id, patient_id, abo, rh_d, antibody_screen_result, draw_datetime, collector_id, validity_expiry (3-day rule), abo_confirmation_status
- antibody_screen (NEW): id, sample_id, method, screen_cell_lot, per-cell results, overall_result, tech_id, datetime
- antibody_identification (NEW): id, sample_id, panel_lot, identified_specificities[]
- patient_antibody_history (NEW, persistent across visits/branches): patient_id, specificity, first_identified_date, source — permanently forces antigen-neg + AHG
- patient_special_requirements (NEW): patient_id, requires_irradiated/cmv_neg/washed/antigen_neg[], reason, set_by, active_from
- crossmatch (NEW): id, sample_id (FK), blood_unit_id (FK), request_id (FK), method (IS/AHG/electronic), result, reagent_lot, tech_id, datetime, electronic_eligibility_snapshot
- emergency_release (NEW): id, request_id, patient_id, units[], physician_id, justification, release_datetime, retrospective_xm_status, signoff_status, signoff_physician_id
- msbos (NEW): procedure_code, branch_id (nullable), order_type (none/T&S/XM), units_count
- transfusion_administration (NEW): id, unit_id, patient_id, bedside_verify_pass, start/stop, vitals, staff_id
- transfusion_reaction (NEW): id, administration_id, reaction_type, severity, workup_result, dat_result
- compatibility_rules: keep static ABO/Rh as a FIRST-PASS filter only; demote from sole arbiter — issue gate now requires sample validity + antibody screen + crossmatch/electronic-eligibility + special-requirement match

## PHP/MySQL feasibility
Vanilla PHP/MySQL/AJAX CAN implement the data model and the deterministic gating logic (sample validity, ABO double-determination, antibody-history flagging, electronic-crossmatch eligibility gates, MSBOS defaults, parent→child lineage via self-referential FK, special-attribute matching at issue). None of this requires features the stack lacks. Where the stack is INADEQUATE for the sellable bar: (1) Validation/compliance burden — electronic crossmatch and any computerized compatibility decision are regulated controls under FDA 21 CFR Part 11 and AABB; the product needs IQ/OQ/PQ validation evidence, change control, and audit that vanilla code does not provide for free. (2) Immutable, tamper-evident audit — 21 CFR Part 11 e-signatures + EU 30-year traceability require append-only/hash-chained audit and controlled record retention; raw MySQL tables are mutable and need application-level enforcement plus DB-level controls (triggers preventing UPDATE/DELETE on audit, periodic integrity hashing). (3) Concurrency at issue — reservation-lock 'intent' is not enough; preventing double-issue of a single unit across branches needs transactional row locking (SELECT ... FOR UPDATE) or optimistic version columns; naive AJAX without DB transactions WILL double-issue under load. (4) Offline-sync of safety-critical history (antibody specificities, special requirements) is a correctness hazard, not a convenience feature; eventual consistency on these fields can kill a patient, so issue must hard-block on stale/low-confidence safety history rather than proceed. (5) Bedside barcode verification needs a hardware/PWA layer beyond plain server-rendered PHP. SUMMARY: feasible to build, but the gap between 'works' and 'sellable to a hospital' is validation, immutable audit, transactional concurrency, and disciplined handling of safety-history under offline sync — these must be engineered explicitly, not assumed.

## Risks & tradeoffs
- ABO/Rh static lookup alone misses: clinically significant non-ABO alloantibodies (anti-K, anti-Fya, anti-Jka, anti-E, etc.) that cause acute/delayed hemolytic reactions; antibodies that have evaniesced below current detection but remain in history; required second ABO determination (WBIT detection); sample validity expiry (3-day rule); and patient special requirements (irradiated/CMV-neg/antigen-neg). Issuing on lookup alone is a patient-safety and regulatory failure.
- Electronic crossmatch is a software-validated control under FDA 21 CFR Part 11 and AABB — selling it means the vendor must provide validation evidence (IQ/OQ/PQ) that the system correctly detects ABO incompatibility and correctly blocks ineligible cases; vanilla PHP/MySQL can implement the logic but the burden is the validation/verification dossier, not the code.
- Sample-validity and antibody-history rules are time- and history-dependent; offline-sync branches risk acting on stale antibody history. A patient flagged with anti-K at branch A must not be issued K-untested units at branch B during a sync gap — the offline queue must treat antibody history and special requirements as high-priority/blocking-replication data, or block group-specific issue when history confidence is stale.
- Irradiation/deglycerolization change unit expiry dynamically; expiry must be recomputed event-driven, not a fixed field, or expired-product issue becomes possible.
- Override pathways (emergency release, electronic-XM downgrade) are necessary but are the most dangerous surfaces; each must carry mandatory identity + reason + e-signature (21 CFR Part 11) and be immutable in audit_logs.
- Component lineage demands true bidirectional traceability with 30-year retention (EU Directive 2002/98/EC); MySQL retention/backup and immutable audit are a procurement/compliance obligation beyond schema design.
