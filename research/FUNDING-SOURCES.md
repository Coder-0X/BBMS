# Blood Bank LIS — Funding Sources & Grant Programs 2025-2026
> Compiled 2026-06-29 | Focus: solo developer / academic team with working prototype

---

## TIER 1 — HIGHEST ACCESSIBILITY (Apply Immediately)

### 1. NLnet Foundation — NGI0 Programs
**What it funds**: Open source internet infrastructure and health tech  
**Amount**: €5,000–€50,000 (milestone-based, typical award €15k–€30k)  
**Deadlines**: Rolling — 1st of every even month (Feb 1, Apr 1, Jun 1, Aug 1, Oct 1, Dec 1)  
**Next deadline**: Check nlnet.nl for current round  
**Eligibility**:
- Any entity globally (individuals, companies, universities, NGOs)
- Project must be 100% open source (OSI-approved license)
- No citizenship or country restriction

**Relevant programs**:
- **NGI0 Commons Fund** — Digital commons and open infrastructure → best fit for blood bank LIS
- **NGI0 Entrust** — Security and trustworthiness of internet services → FHIR/HL7 interop angle
- **NGI Search** — Search/discovery technologies → less relevant

**How to apply**: https://nlnet.nl/apply/  
**Tips**:
- Keep it simple and concrete: "open-source blood bank LIS for resource-limited settings"
- Emphasize open standard compliance (FHIR R4, Code 128, ISO 15189)
- Break project into clear milestones with deliverables
- Payments are milestone-based (no upfront lump sum)

**Fit**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ EXCELLENT — This is the best starting point for a solo developer

### 2. AABB Foundation — Research & Education Grants
**What it funds**: Transfusion medicine research, education, and patient safety  
**Amount**: $25,000–$75,000 (research grants); smaller education grants available  
**Deadline**: Annual cycle — typically Spring (March/April application window)  
**Eligibility**:
- AABB members and affiliated institutions
- Academic, clinical, and industry applicants
- Individual investigators welcome (no institution requirement for all grants)

**Grant types**:
- Clinical/Basic Science Research Grant (up to $75k)
- Education Grant (up to $20k)
- Patient Safety Grant — directly relevant to LIS/error prevention
- Career Development Award (if you are early-career clinician or scientist)

**How to apply**: https://www.aabb.org/research/aabb-foundation  
**Tips**:
- Frame as "patient safety technology" — WBIT prevention, electronic crossmatch, bedside verification
- Cite SHOT data on near-misses and serious adverse reactions
- AABB membership is required (~$200/year individual membership)

**Fit**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ HIGH — directly in-scope; requires membership

### 3. Gates Foundation — Grand Challenges Explorations
**What it funds**: Global health innovations for low/middle-income countries  
**Amount**: Phase 1: $100,000 (no strings); Phase 2: up to $1,000,000 (competitive)  
**Deadlines**: Multiple rounds per year — check grandchallenges.org for open calls  
**Eligibility**:
- Global — any country, any organization type, individuals welcome
- Phase 1 is intentionally low-barrier (2-page application)
- No preliminary data required for Phase 1

**Relevant challenge areas**:
- "Creating New Interventions for Global Health" — digital diagnostics, LIS
- "Global Health Equity" — access to blood safety infrastructure in LMICs
- Occasionally specific calls for blood safety / transfusion medicine

**How to apply**: https://gcgh.grandchallenges.org/  
**Framing strategy**:
- "Open-source blood bank LIS for sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia where paper-based systems dominate"
- Emphasize: 118,000 deaths/year from unsafe blood transfusions (WHO stat)
- Key differentiator: zero licensing cost, deployable on $50/month server, FHIR-ready

**Fit**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ HIGH — LMIC angle is very compelling to Gates

## TIER 2 — MEDIUM ACCESSIBILITY (Requires More Preparation)

### 4. NIH NHLBI SBIR/STTR (US Entities Only)
**What it funds**: Small business research in heart, lung, blood, and sleep disorders  
**Amount**:
- Phase I (R43): up to $250,000 over 6 months
- Phase II (R44): up to $1,500,000 over 2 years
- STTR: same amounts, requires university partnership (minimum 30% effort)

**Deadlines**: Rolling (3 submission windows per year: Jan, Apr, Sep)  
**Parent announcements**: PA-24-222 (SBIR R43/R44), PA-24-223 (STTR R41/R42)  
**Eligibility**:
- **US small business** (< 500 employees, majority US-owned) for SBIR
- University or non-profit partner required for STTR
- PI must be affiliated with the small business or university

**Blood safety angle**: Transfusion medicine informatics, blood supply management, donor safety technology  
**URL**: https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/sbir  
**Required**: US LLC/corporation + significant preparation (Specific Aims, Background, Innovation, Approach)

**Fit**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ HIGH (for US-based developers)

---

### 5. NIH NLM R21 Exploratory Research (Via University)
**What it funds**: Biomedical informatics, health data standards, clinical informatics  
**Amount**: Up to $275,000 over 2 years (R21 exploratory grant)  
**Deadlines**: Standard NIH dates — February 16, June 16, October 16  
**Eligibility**:
- US academic institutions (requires university affiliation)
- Graduate students can be co-investigators
- International collaboration allowed as sub-awards

**FHIR-based blood bank LIS is directly in scope for NLM biomedical informatics**  
**URL**: https://www.nlm.nih.gov/ep/GrantTrainIntro.html  

**Fit**: ⭐⭐⭐ MEDIUM (requires US university partner)

### 6. Wellcome Trust — Digital Technologies
**What it funds**: Digital health infrastructure, open science, data standards  
**Amount**:
- Discovery Awards: up to £200,000 (2 years)
- Investigator Awards: up to £3,000,000 (5 years, for established researchers)
- Seed Awards: £50,000–£100,000 (shorter pilot)

**Deadline**: Rolling for some schemes; specific windows for others  
**Eligibility**:
- Primarily requires host institution (university, research institute, hospital)
- Can be any country where Wellcome has presence
- Strong preference for researchers with institutional affiliation

**URL**: https://wellcome.org/grant-funding/schemes  
**Tips**:
- Frame as "open digital infrastructure for blood safety in low-resource settings"
- Emphasize open source and reproducibility — Wellcome cares deeply about open science
- Find a UK or international research partner

**Fit**: ⭐⭐⭐ MEDIUM (needs institution affiliation)

### 7. EU Horizon Europe — Health Cluster (Consortium Required)
**What it funds**: Health research and innovation in Europe and globally  
**Budget**: €8.3 billion for health (2021–2027)  
**Amount**: RIA (Research & Innovation Action): typically €3–15M; IA (Innovation Action): similar  
**Deadlines**: Published in annual Work Programme (2026 calls active now)  
**Eligibility**:
- Minimum 3 partners from 3 different EU member states
- Non-EU partners allowed as associated partners
- SMEs and startups are welcome as consortium members

**Cascade funding** (more accessible):
- Sub-grants via EIC Pathfinder projects: €50k–€200k
- European Health Data Space implementation grants
- Digital Europe Programme for health data infrastructure

**URL**: https://hadea.ec.europa.eu/programmes/horizon-europe/health_en  
**Strategy**: Join a consortium as a technical partner (bring the LIS); university leads

**Fit**: ⭐⭐⭐ MEDIUM (requires EU consortium formation)

## KEY STATISTICS TO INCLUDE IN ALL APPLICATIONS

- **118,000+ deaths annually** from unsafe blood transfusions (WHO 2023)
- **>50% of blood transfusions** in low-income countries are unsafe (WHO)
- **Only 35%** of low-income country blood services have functional BECS (WHO)
- **SHOT 2023**: 3,744 adverse transfusion reports in the UK alone; 1,119 WBIT events
- **C:T ratio target**: 2.0 or below; poor blood bank software contributes to overordering
- **Commercial BECS cost**: $200,000–$2,000,000+ for implementation (our project: $0 licensing)
- **Code 128 vs ISBT 128**: Our approach is 100% free; commercial ISBT 128 requires ICCBBA paid membership
